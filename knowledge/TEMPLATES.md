# knowledge/ — business context files

These three files are the ONLY place business-specific information lives.
Skills and agents load them at runtime. To reuse business-os for another
business, replace these files and nothing else.

- [business-profile.md](business-profile.md) — who the business is, domain, target communities
- [voice-spec.md](voice-spec.md) — writing voice/register rules for public replies
- [claims-allowed.md](claims-allowed.md) — facts/figures allowed to be asserted publicly
