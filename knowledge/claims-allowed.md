# Claims Allowed in Public Replies

Only these facts/figures may be asserted. Anything not listed: say what
you'd check instead. Update as verified facts accumulate.

**Marketplace economics (public knowledge, safe):**
- Major delivery marketplaces charge roughly 15–30% commission depending
  on plan tier; 30% is the typical top marketplace tier
- Inflating delivery-menu prices 15–20% over dine-in is standard practice
- Marketplace customer data belongs to the marketplace, not the restaurant

**Operator-experience framing (allowed):**
- "we onboard restaurants" / "the restaurants I work with" — yes
- Anonymized patterns from client experience — yes, no client names

**Never assert:**
- Client names or identifiable client stories (even flattering ones)
- Specific AIOrders pricing, features, or performance numbers
- Revenue/growth claims about AIOrders
- Any statistic you cannot source to this file or a neutral third party
