# G1 — ENG-016 rescope, after your `changed` answer

## What changed since the last G1

Your reply to the first G1 wasn't an edit — it was a complete replacement
engineering spec (full text preserved in `inbox/2026-08-29-eng016-g1-scope.md`'s
`## Decision` section). We read it fully, verified it against the live
code in all three repos, and it changes the shape of this ticket
substantially: it asks for the catering-specific tiered pricing model the
first PRD explicitly named as a *non-goal*, plus the owner-side quote
editing the first PRD explicitly *deferred*. Both are real, both are

## Four things your rewrite changes that are worth knowing before you decide

1. **Your rewrite removes the SMS/email link.** The original ask was a
   link sent to the customer; your rewrite puts the whole selector
   directly on the public catering page instead, and SMS/email becomes
   just a summary sent *after* they submit. We built to this — it's
   simpler — but flagging it in case that wasn't a deliberate change.
2. **The fulfillment dropdown and guest-count field already exist** (as
   Pickup / Delivery / Live Catering / Party Hall / Food Truck, flag-gated
   per restaurant) — your three proposed values (On-site / Pickup&Delivery
   / Drop-off Trays) don't map cleanly onto those. We'll need to decide
   whether to remap existing restaurants' data or add your three
   alongside the current five.
3. **The five status columns are hardcoded in eight different files**, not
   a simple list — every new stage we add touches all eight, and going
   from five columns to seven is a real layout change to the board, not
   just adding an option.
4. **Not every restaurant can use this.** Menus are structured data only
   for restaurants whose menu is built as a page on our platform. A
   restaurant that uploaded a PDF menu or embeds an external ordering
   widget has nothing to build a selector from — the order form stays
   exactly as it is today for them.

## The stage-count mismatch in your own spec

Your stage matrix names three target stages (`Quote Generated`, `Contact
Requested`, `Quote Viewed`), but your "Kanban Status Enum" instruction
only lists two to actually add (`Quote Generated`, `Contact Requested`).
We're building the two your enum instruction names. `Quote Viewed` — "the
customer opened the link but hasn't submitted" — can't be built as
described because your rewrite removes the link it depends on (point 1
above): there's no card to move into that column until a submission
creates one. It becomes buildable in Piece 3 below, once a resent quote
is a real per-record link a customer can open. Say the word if you'd
rather have view-tracking sooner and we'll design it against page visits
instead — that's a different, and more expensive, build.

## Recommendation: build Piece 1 now, hold Pieces 2 and 3

**Piece 1 (this ticket, `L`, $0/month) — build now.** A customer picks a
fulfillment option and guest count, selects dishes by category with
quantities and a note each (no prices shown), and either submits or asks
for a callback instead. The request lands on the board already in
`Quote Generated` or `Contact Requested`, and you see the exact order
itemized instead of a paragraph of free text. This alone removes the
actual manual step — guessing what the customer wants over the phone —
without waiting on anything else.

**Piece 2 (not filed) — catering package & price book, `L`.** You'd
author, per restaurant, a package (a base rate, how many selections from
each category are included, and the upcharge for extra ones) — this is
what makes "Choose 2 appetizers, +$3/person" actually show a real price.
Nothing in your spec says who builds and maintains that price list per
restaurant as menus change, and nothing like it exists in the product
today. **We'd like your answer on that before filing this piece** — it's
the one part of your rewrite that creates ongoing work rather than
removing it.

**Piece 3 (not filed) — owner-side edit, resend, and view tracking,
`M`–`L`.** Editing a quote's selections and sending a revised one by
SMS/email, with a real per-quote link this time (Piece 1 has no quote to
send yet, Piece 2 supplies the price to edit). `Quote Viewed` lives here.

## Cost

it that whole time.
