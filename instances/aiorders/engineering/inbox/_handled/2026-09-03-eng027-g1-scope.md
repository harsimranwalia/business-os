**That you want this one now:** you answered **yes** on 2026-09-01 to

**Understood as:** `ENG-006` gave you one identity per verified phone.
`ENG-007` gave every restaurant an effective-dated earn rate — and that
table holds **zero rows today, because nothing reads it.** This ticket is
the piece that joins them: a permanent, append-only record of points
earned, per diner per restaurant, credited automatically when an online
order is recorded and manually when staff enter a dine-in amount, each at
whichever rate was in effect at that moment. Balances readable per diner
per restaurant. **Nothing can be spent** — points only go up. Redemption
and QR are ticket 4; admin lookup and manual adjustment are ticket 5.

**One rider to answer here rather than at a second gate** (same bar
`ENG-015`/`ENG-016` used):

**What does the earn % apply to?** Your request said "give the customers
points for online ordering and dine in" and `ENG-007` stored a percentage,
but nobody has ever said a percentage *of what*. An order record carries a
bill total (tax and fees included), a separate tip, a cart figure and a
discount. **Proposed: the pre-tax, post-discount food subtotal — excluding
tax, fees, delivery and tip.** That's the common restaurant convention and
it avoids paying diners points on the government's share. Correcting this
now is free; correcting it after diners have balances is not.

**Three things checked against live code, not assumed:**

1. **There is no order-completion signal in the system.** The CloudWaitress
   `order_new` webhook is the *only* thing that ever writes an order, and
   nothing anywhere updates or deletes one. So points accrue when an order
   is **placed**, not when it's fulfilled — and a cancelled or refunded
   order keeps its points until someone corrects it by hand (ticket 5). If
   you want accrual on fulfilment instead, that signal has to be built
   first and this ticket goes from `M` to `L`.
2. **Walletly holds nothing locally.** The integration is a pure
   pass-through proxy to the vendor's API — every existing point balance
   lives on their servers, not in your database. **Migrating them is out
   of scope here** and I think that's right (it's a goodwill decision, not
   an engineering one) — but it's **time-sensitive**: once the contract
   lapses, an export may be impossible at any price. If you want existing
   balances honoured, say so and it gets filed as its own ticket before the
   vendor goes.
3. **No second repo is needed.** Dine-in manual entry lands as an
   authenticated API action on `brand-portal`, the staff-facing surface
   that already exists in `aiorders-api` with a working restaurant-scoped
   auth gate. The staff screen that will eventually drive it is already
   captured as deferred work in
   `agents/product-manager/specs/loyalty-program-frontend-understanding.md`.
   Frontend stays out of the whole sequence, as you instructed.

**Not doing** (this ticket): spending points, redemption, or QR
issuance/scanning (ticket 4); admin lookup, cross-restaurant support view,
or manual ledger adjustment/void (ticket 5); migrating Walletly balances;
any frontend anywhere; point expiry; backfilling points for existing
orders; setting real rates for real restaurants.

## Problem

with nothing to award and a rate table with zero rows that nothing reads.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `[stated]` Given a diner with a platform identity places an online order
   at a configured restaurant, when the order is recorded, then points are
   credited to their balance at that restaurant at the **online** rate in
   effect at that moment.
2. `[stated]` Given a staff member with access to a restaurant records a
   dine-in amount for a diner with a platform identity, when submitted,
   then points are credited at the **dine-in** rate in effect at that
   moment.
3. `[inferred]` Given points are credited by either path, when the entry is
   written, then it permanently records the spend amount, the rate applied,
   the source (online or dine-in), and what caused it.
4. `[inferred]` Given a rate change afterwards, when a past entry is read
   back, then it still reports the rate applied at the time and the same
   points — the change has no effect on it.
5. `[inferred]` Given a diner has entries at more than one restaurant, when
   one restaurant's balance is read, then it equals exactly that
   restaurant's entries and is unaffected by any other's.
6. `[inferred]` Given the same online order is delivered more than once,
   when the repeat is processed, then no additional points are credited.
7. `[inferred]` Given a restaurant that reads as not enrolled, when an
   order is recorded or a dine-in amount submitted, then no points are
   credited, no entry is created, and the dine-in caller is told why.
8. `[inferred]` Given an online order from a customer with no linked
   platform identity, when it is recorded, then the order is recorded
   exactly as today and no points are credited.
9. `[proposed]` Given accrual fails for any reason, when an online order
   arrives, then the order is still recorded — accrual can never block or
   fail order recording.
10. `[proposed]` Given a staff member submits a dine-in amount for a
    restaurant they lack access to, when submitted, then it is rejected and
    no entry is created.
11. `[proposed]` Given a dine-in amount that is zero, negative, or not a
    number, when submitted, then it is rejected with a clear reason.

## Non-goals

Spending points, redemption, QR issuance or scanning (ticket 4); admin and
support surfaces — lookup, cross-restaurant view, manual adjustment/void
(ticket 5); migrating existing balances out of Walletly; any frontend in
any repo, including the restaurant-portal staff screen for dine-in entry;
point expiry; pooled or transferable balances; backfilling points for
existing orders; setting real rates for real restaurants; adding an order
completion, cancellation, or refund path. Full list in the PRD.

## Risks and unknowns

- **Accrual at placement, not fulfilment, with no reversal path** — no
  completion or cancellation signal exists in the system to hang one on.
  Cancelled orders keep their points until corrected by hand (ticket 5).
- **Walletly balances vanish when the vendor is switched off** — nothing is
  stored locally. Time-sensitive; out of scope here, worth its own ticket
  if you want them honoured.
- **The earn base is being decided by inference** — the rider above. Wrong
  here means every balance is wrong by a consistent, invisible margin.
- **Dine-in amounts are unverifiable** — no POS integration exists; staff
  type a number and the platform credits it. Detectable only once ticket
  5's audit surfaces exist.
- **The dine-in path has no operator until the portal work lands** — it
  will be exercised by tests and nothing else, possibly for months.
- **First money-adjacent write path this department has added to
  `aiorders-api`** (the highest-blast-radius project on the registry), and
  it hangs off the live production order webhook. Criterion 9 exists
  because of this.
- **PIPEDA**, unchanged from `ENG-006` — a per-restaurant spend history tied
  to a verified phone is more sensitive than an order log, and it's being
  built before any consent or opt-out surface exists.
