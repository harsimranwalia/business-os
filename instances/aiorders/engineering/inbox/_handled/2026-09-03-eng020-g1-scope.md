# G1 — Marketing ROI reporting (ENG-020)

## Readback

**You said:** "AI SEO no way to track if its useful or working on brand
dasboard. no roi from AI SEO or tracking how and where the customers are
coming from . we have started to install microsoft clarity on restaurant
website but can demostrate how aiorders is helping their business with
increasing sales or roi or any other metrics"

**Understood as:** restaurant owners have no way to see, anywhere in the
product, whether AI-generated SEO is doing anything — no view of where
customers come from, and no link from that traffic to orders or revenue.
Microsoft Clarity (behaviour analytics — heatmaps, session replay) was never
going to answer "is this making us money," and nothing inside AIOrders does
either today. This is a reporting gap, not a capture gap — the data
(`utm_source`, `first_touch_source`, `first_referrer`) is already written on
every customer row by every signup path this platform has; nothing reads it
back out to an owner.

**Second reading:** no material divergence. The PM's reading (grounded in
live code across `aiorders-admin-hub`, `config-site-builder`, `aiorders-api`,
`restaurant-portal`) and a blind architect reading independently converged
on the same shape — a per-restaurant view joining traffic source to
order/revenue outcomes, with Clarity named as the wrong tool for this
question.

**Assumed, correctable here:**
- "AI SEO" = the existing admin-hub "Generate with AI" SEO tab applied to a
  restaurant's `config-site-builder` website — not paid ads, not Google
  Business Profile.
- "but can demostrate" read as "but [cannot] demonstrate" — a missing
  negation both readings independently converged on.
- "Brand dashboard" = the brand portal (`restaurant-portal`), same surface
  `ENG-014`/`ENG-015`/`ENG-019` already use that name for.
- Primary audience is the restaurant owner; a staff-facing rollup for
  AIOrders' own retention/sales use is real but proposed as a follow-on, not
  built here.
- "ROI" is scoped down to revenue by traffic source/channel, not a true
  return-on-investment ratio (that needs each restaurant's subscription cost
  on the other side of the ledger, which no analytics surface here has) —
  same narrowing this board already applied to `ENG-019`'s coupon-only ROI.
  Microsoft Clarity itself is proposed out of scope.

**Not doing (this ticket):** Clarity integration, a true ROI ratio,
isolating AI-generated SEO specifically from organic traffic generally (no
record exists today of which restaurants have AI vs. manual SEO), a
staff-facing all-restaurants rollup, or any change to the existing
`Analytics` (influencer) or `Reports` pages.

**Flagged, not a divergence:** the architect's blind reading raised
attribution honesty (a last-click number will overstate SEO's specific
effect — acceptance criterion 4 exists because of this), cross-domain
attribution completeness (the existing tracking script's online-ordering
wiring is a per-deployment task, not guaranteed live everywhere), privacy/
legal exposure (PIPEDA and Quebec's Law 25 — session recording and even
first-party UTM capture touch consent questions, worth a real check),
absence of a historical baseline for existing customers, small-restaurant
traffic noise, and tenant isolation (this codebase's confirmed history via
`ENG-015` — acceptance criterion 5 exists because of that precedent). Full
detail: `agents/product-manager/specs/ENG-020-marketing-roi-attribution-reporting.md`.

## Recommendation

Build now, scoped as above. `M` — no new data model (the attribution
columns already exist and are already being written), no new vendor.
Extends the already-wired `analytics` edge function plus a new report view
on an existing page. $0/month expected. Full PRD:
`agents/product-manager/specs/ENG-020-marketing-roi-attribution-reporting.md`.
