## Readback

**You said (answering the standing question this ticket comes from,
`inbox/2026-08-29-eng023-frequency-question.md`):** "reading b, frequent is
same restaurant over time"

**Understood as:** On the brand portal's Feedback page, alongside the flat
list `ENG-023` is adding status/notes to, a restaurant should see a summary
of which kinds of issues recur for their own location — grouped and ranked
by frequency, reflecting their history over time, and never compared
against or blended with other restaurants' data.

**Requirements:**
1. `[confirmed]` A restaurant sees their own recurring-issues summary,
   grouped by category, ranked by frequency.
2. `[confirmed]` Scoped to one restaurant's own history over time — not a
   snapshot, and never cross-restaurant.
3. `[inferred]` Grouping uses the feedback's existing `sub_type`/`nature`
   fields rather than new free-text categorization — see Evidence.

**Assumed, and worth correcting if wrong:**
- Only actionable natures (complaint, negative, suggestion, bug) are framed
  as "issues" to recur — compliments aren't presented as a problem, even
  though the same grouping mechanically covers them too. Say so if you'd
  rather see all natures in one recurring-issues view.
- "Over time" means the restaurant's available history (their own Feedback
  page already fetches all of it, unfiltered by date) rather than a fixed
  recent window — exact windowing, if any, is left to the architect.

resolution of a standing question that itself already went through the
