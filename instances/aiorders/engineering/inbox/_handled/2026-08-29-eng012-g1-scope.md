## Problem

Staff have no system of record for restaurant-reported issues, and no way
to see open-ticket load per restaurant when prioritizing.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `[confirmed]` Given a restaurant with no tickets, when staff create one,
   then it appears with a default open status and persists.
2. `[confirmed]` Given an open ticket, when staff assign/reassign an
   owner, then it persists and is visible.
3. `[confirmed]` Given an open ticket, when staff change its status, then
   the change persists and is reflected wherever tickets are listed.
4. `[confirmed]` Given a restaurant with N open tickets, its Brands-page
   row shows an open count of N.
5. `[inferred]` A closed ticket no longer counts toward that total.
6. `[inferred]` A non-staff request to any ticket path is rejected by the
   existing admin-portal authorization gate.

## Non-goals

Restaurant/public submission; SLAs/escalation/notifications; external
helpdesk integration; full audit history per ticket.
