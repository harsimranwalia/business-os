# G1 — Client stage & health visibility on the Brands admin page — plus stage filtering

## Readback

**You said:** "on the admin panel we are unable to see if the restaurant is
our client or what stage he is at on the brands page we have. the admin
staff should be able to filter according to the stage of the client so
they can prioritize the clients based on the stage they are at . also the
health of restaurant and maybe tickets" (full input preserved in the
ticket's own `## Input`,
`agents/eng-manager/board/ENG-011-client-stage-health-visibility.md`)

**Understood as:** The Brands admin page doesn't show whether a restaurant
is a current client or what stage it's at, and staff can't filter by
stage to prioritize their work. You also want a health signal on the same
page. **This ticket covers the stage + filter + a minimal health signal.**
"Tickets" is a standing, non-blocking question already with you separately
(`inbox/2026-08-29-eng011-tickets-source-question.md`), since it swings
scope by roughly an order of magnitude and isn't answerable from the
request text — no ticket/support system exists anywhere in either repo
today, confirmed by checking rather than assuming.

**Requirements for this ticket:**
1. `[stated]` Staff can see each restaurant's client stage on the Brands
   page.
2. `[proposed]` The stage itself answers "is this our client" — no
   separate client flag.
3. `[stated]` Staff can filter the Brands page by stage.
4. `[stated]` Staff can see a restaurant-health signal on the same page.

**Assumed, and worth correcting if wrong:**
- **Proposed stage taxonomy**, grounded in columns that already exist but
  aren't surfaced (`onboarding_step`, `is_active` on the `brands` table):
  Onboarding → Live/Active → Inactive/Churned. Not stated — a starting
  point for you to correct here; exact naming is the architect's call at
  design time either way.
- **Health, for this ticket, is a minimal activity-based signal** (derived
  from existing order data, no new integration) — not a weighted score
  with thresholds or alerts. That fuller version is explicitly a
  non-goal, named so it doesn't get built by accident or expected by
  surprise.
- **"Is our client" is fully answered by the stage field** — proposed as
  one field rather than two, partly because a second, separately-set
  "client" flag risks drifting out of sync with stage over time (a risk
  the architect's independent reading raised).

**Not doing (this ticket):** support-ticket count/indicator of any kind
(standing question, separate); a scored/weighted/alerting health model;
any change to the separately-raised agency/reseller admin-scoping
request; a staff-editable stage taxonomy; leads/pre-brand records.

## Recommendation

**Build ENG-011 now.** The stage + filter half is exactly what you asked
for in plain terms and is independently useful the moment it ships —
staff can start prioritizing by stage immediately. The health signal adds
real value at no extra cost (derived from data already on the platform).
Neither carries a one-way door: no new vendor, no new datastore, reuses
the existing admin-auth gate.

## Problem

Admin staff can't tell which restaurants are actual clients versus
earlier-stage or inactive ones, and can't see restaurant health, so the
Brands page can't be worked in priority order today.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `[stated]` Given the Brands page, when staff view the list, then each
   row shows the restaurant's current stage.
2. `[proposed]` Given a restaurant's stage, staff can read from it alone
   whether it's a current client.
3. `[stated]` Given the Brands page, when staff apply a stage filter, then
   only restaurants in the selected stage(s) are shown.
4. `[inferred]` Given an applied filter, when staff clear it, then the
   full list returns.
5. `[proposed]` Given a restaurant with order history, when staff view its
   row, then a minimal health indicator is shown.
6. `[proposed]` Given a non-staff request to the Brands page or its
   filter, then it's rejected by the existing admin-portal authorization
   gate.

## Non-goals

Support-ticket visibility (standing question, separate); a
scored/weighted health model; the separate agency/reseller scoping
request; a configurable stage taxonomy; leads/pre-brand records.

## Risks and unknowns

- Stage taxonomy proposed, not confirmed — correctable here at no cost,
  nothing built yet.
- "Health" could grow past a minimal signal if that's not what you meant
  — flagged now rather than found mid-build.
- "Tickets" confirmed genuinely unbuilt anywhere in either repo — could be
  small or large depending on your answer to the standing question.
