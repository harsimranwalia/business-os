# G1 — Autopilot nurture for the presignup sales lead pipeline — stage-triggered email/SMS

## Readback

**You said:** "no autopilot on admin panel for our sales staff/ resellers to
use . how can we demonstrate to a client what we sell if we dont have it
for us. have a proper fully demonstration account on how all aiorders work.
also autopilot nurturing for resellers/sales/admin staff on admin panel
which works based on stages update/ auto nurturing ."

**Understood as:** This raw request bundles two separable asks — this
ticket covers only the stage-triggered nurture half (the demonstration-
account half is `ENG-018`, filed alongside it). A website "become a client"
lead sits in a flat table with no stage and no follow-up mechanism. This
ticket gives it a staff-settable stage, and moving it into a nurture-flagged
stage automatically sends a templated email/SMS, the same way the platform
already automates marketing for a restaurant's own customers — pointed at
AIOrders' own sales pipeline instead. This restates and broadens a standing
question already answered under `ENG-013` (same mechanism, approved
2026-08-29T11:46:34) — treated as confirmed evidence for the core mechanism,
though this ticket still owes its own G1 since it broadens scope (taxonomy,
reseller non-goal, consent gap, trigger stages) beyond what that question
covered.

**Requirements:**
1. `[confirmed]` Every lead carries a stage, staff-settable, defaulting to
   New.
2. `[confirmed]` Moving a lead into a nurture-flagged stage (proposed:
   Contacted, Interested) sends a templated email/SMS automatically,
   reusing existing send services.
3. `[proposed]` Every automated send is logged and visible to staff.
4. `[inferred]` A lead that has since signed up for real stops receiving
   nurture messages.
5. `[proposed]` The lead-capture form gains a consent notice before this
   ships — nurture sends rest on explicit consent, not an unexamined
   implied-consent assumption.

**Scoped OUT, deliberately:**
- **Reseller (`partner-admin`/`partner-user`) access** — no mechanism
  anywhere attributes a lead to a specific reseller (no referral code, no
  `partner_id`); granting access today would mean every reseller sees every
  lead platform-wide, the same cross-tenant shape `ENG-015` is currently
  fixing elsewhere. Real attribution is separate, non-trivial work — this
  ticket ships the nurture engine for internal `admin`/`sub-admin` staff
  only.
- **Extending nurture to the Brands-page (`ENG-011`) or Foodswipe-funnel
  (`ENG-013`) stage fields** — different, already-signed-up entities, each
  with its own in-flight ticket.

**Evidence checked, not assumed.** The `leads` table has no stage/status
column and its capture form records no consent flag at all, unlike the
catering flow's explicit `consent_sms`/`consent_email`. The existing
`autopilot`/`communication_templates` model is hard-scoped to
`restaurant_id` and a closed set of customer-lifecycle triggers — a
presignup lead has no `restaurant_id` and isn't a customer, so it can't be
enrolled through that data model as-is; this ticket builds a parallel,
lead-shaped layer instead, reusing only the underlying send services.
`outgoing-communications`'s `actor: 'admin'` route exists in the router but
its handlers are unimplemented `TODO` stubs — not working prior art.
AIOrders is a Canadian business; CASL governs automated commercial
email/SMS, and "filled out a contact form" is a materially weaker consent
basis than an explicit opt-in — hence requirement 5.

**Second reading (blind architect, no repo access) converged on the core** —
independently read the raw request as two bundled asks, independently named
reseller/sales scoping as a real prerequisite, and additionally, unprompted,
raised the CASL consent exposure — checked against the live form and
confirmed real. No material divergence.

**Non-goals:** reseller access; nurture on the Brands-page or
Foodswipe-funnel stage fields; a new outbound channel beyond email/SMS;
retroactively messaging the existing lead backlog; extending the
restaurant-facing `communication_templates` model to leads. Full detail:
`agents/product-manager/specs/ENG-017-presignup-lead-nurture-autopilot.md`.
